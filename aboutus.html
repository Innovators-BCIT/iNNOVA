iNNOVA

About us

iNNOVA is an online appointment platform that connects customers with available booking slots across different service industries. By making last-minute appointments easier to discover and book, we help customers find convenient services while giving businesses an opportunity to fill otherwise unused time slots.

Our mission

Our mission is to reduce wasted appointment time by connecting businesses with customers looking for available bookings. We aim to help service providers recover lost revenue, keep schedules full, and make finding and booking appointments easier for everyone.
